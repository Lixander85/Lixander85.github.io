JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What JARVIS is

JARVIS is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on its owner's computer and uploads videos that the owner places in a folder to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What Google data it uses

JARVIS asks for one permission: upload videos to your YouTube account (youtube.upload). It uses this only to upload the video files you choose, with the title and description you give them. It does not read, change or delete anything else in your Google or YouTube account.

Where your data is stored

Your Google sign-in token is saved only on your own computer, in the JARVIS folder. JARVIS has no server, collects no analytics, and does not send your data to anyone other than Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

JARVIS does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

Removing access

You can remove JARVIS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the token.json file from the JARVIS folder.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for JARVIS.
